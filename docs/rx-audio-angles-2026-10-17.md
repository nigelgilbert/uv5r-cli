# Angle reports (four research agents, 2026-10-17)

## A. Measurement / divide-and-conquer agent
- No published UV-5R jack voltage; RigPix rates speaker output 600 mW at 5% THD (~2.2 V rms into 8 ohm at full volume). Open-squelch static at mid volume is hundreds of mV.
- 2.5 mm jack: tip SPK+, ring TX data, sleeve ground (miklor, pinouts.ru). TDA2822 amp powered through Q19 only when squelch opens (worldwidedx). A bent internal jack contact can mute the speaker while passing no signal (CHIRP list thread 65RQFKBAUMUFT5MYR36LXZWQVPA7CMW6).
- Inference: jack output is single-ended and capacitor-coupled, DC at tip ~0 V (NOTE: contradicted by agent C, which cites AIOC issue 48: ~3.6 V DC on the speaker line while squelch is open).
- Ordered tests: (1) plug out, MONI, internal speaker; (2) power cycle, SQL 0 from keypad; (3) AIOC floor with plug removed at 1x and 16x; (4) volume knob sweep while capturing per-second RMS [starred]; (5) seat/wiggle test while capturing [starred]; (6) earbuds or known-good speaker-mic on the jack; (7) voice prompt as known source at 16x, play wav back by ear; (8) deterministic RF source: FM broadcast mode, NOAA 162.4-162.55, second radio with 1 kHz tone on 462.5625; (9) multimeter AC at C14 pad (expect 0.1-1 V under MONI), DC at tip, TDA2822 Vcc; (10) scope; (11) swap radios.

## B. UV-5R firmware / EEPROM agent
- CHIRP uv5r.py decoding: radio 0x0E20 = settings.squelch (bench write targets the right byte). Radio 0x1F60 = squelch_new.vhf.sql0..9 (bench 00,17..1F; fixture 00,74..7C; sql0 = 0 in both). Radio 0x1F80 = squelch_new.uhf (bench row non-monotonic 05 03 02 01 01 01 02 02 02 02). 0x1EC0-0x1ECF undocumented in CHIRP. CHIRP treats 0x1F00-0x1F5F, 0x1F70-0x1F7F, 0x1F90-0x1FBF as per-unit data and refuses to upload them unless the image matches the radio, so differences between two units there are expected. 0x0F40-0x0F4D undefined in CHIRP; FM band strings there point to a newer firmware variant with 65-108 MHz FM.
- Hypotheses: (1★) something other than carrier squelch mutes audio: RX CTCSS/DCS on VFO A (read 0x0F16-0x0F17), or stale wrong-firmware image (CHIRP #11045 fixed with UseAtOwnRisk restore; #2155 lists RX tone and early N5R BEEP-off bug). (2★) different layout/hardware variant: CHIRP disables HN5RV011 aux writes because some units break when aux is written (#10712, #10505; in #10505 one user's squelch stayed near level 2). (3★) receiver produces no noise (front end/PLL/demod fault); test by MONI on internal speaker with plug out, and by capturing a voice prompt vs static. (4) aux reads unreliable: CHIRP says new radios return bad aux data unless a block outside aux is read first; in #10505 reading 0x1EC0 right after 0x1E80 returned 0xFF. (5) aux thresholds corrupted (weak: sql0 = 0). (6) battery save gating (weak). (7) TDR / FM radio bit (bit 4 of 0x0E40) / STE / ROGER (weak). (8) no known HN5RV011 RX audio bug.

## C. UV-5R jack / output hardware agent
- Facts: AIOC input AC-coupled through C14 4.7 uF, R5 5.1k, R16 5.1k to ADC_BIAS, C11 4.7 nF; load ~10k; R160 1k loses ~0.8 dB; plug spacing 12 mm on AIOC. UV-5R speaker line carries ~3.6 V DC while squelch is open (AIOC issue #48). In carrier-squelch mode the amp U4 is muted by switching its supply through Q19 (kc9hi tech discussion PDF).
- Hypotheses: (1★) J301 tip spring not touching the plug tip (oxidation, bent leaf); test DC at AIOC tip pad before C14 to sleeve: ~3.6 V good, ~0 V open. (2★) open R160, cracked tip solder joint, broken trace. (3★) plug does not seat to full depth: recess ridge, shell, 12 mm vs 11 mm spacing (electronics-notes); KC9HI calls an unseated 2-pin plug the #2 Baofeng fault, fix is trimming the shell; AIOC maintainer lists jack spacing as a cause (issue 106). (4) AIOC plug tip length/diameter (PG-204P / Adafruit 1800). (5) DC-load plug detection (ruled out: speaker mutes). (6) DC clamping (ruled out: C14). (7) Q19/MUTE keeps amp off (weak). (8) hardware revision wires jack differently (unsupported). (9) pinout reversal (electronics-notes is the outlier). Leads: AIOC #126 wrong-bias behaviour at 1x on v1.2 hardware until switching to 2x; early N5R-20 muted RX when BEEP off.

## D. AIOC firmware / USB / macOS capture agent
- Facts: PB2 used when gain=1, input range 0-6.6 V, divider 1/2 so full scale at tip is +-3.3 V; 1x IRQ reads ADC2 12-bit left-aligned; 0xD3 is linear mic volume, 0xFFFF unity. -62 dBFS = 26 int16 rms = 1.6 LSB = 1.3 mV at pin = 2.6 mV rms at tip; 300 mV rms speaker audio would read ~-21 dBFS at 1x.
- Hypotheses: (1★) AIOC reports the tip faithfully and the tip really carries ~2.6 mV (codes exact multiples of 16; 6 dB per step; squelch changes it; top-end rolloff matches C11 pole at 13.3 kHz). (2★) ffmpeg avfoundation drops buffers, explaining short captures and the 0.8% pitch error (ffmpeg tickets #11398, #10038; nonstrict.eu blog). Cannot explain low level. Test: count samples vs wall time; read 0xD2 RECRATE and 0xD8 PLAYRATE during capture. (3★) USB disconnect from RF on the USB cable if keyed (AIOC README known issue); test with `log show --predicate 'subsystem=="com.apple.iokit"'`. (4) TCC/volume/aggregate device: ruled out. (5) 1.4.x reads a different pin at 1x: ruled out. (6) AIOC loading the amp: ruled out. (7) macOS feedback quirk: playback only. (8) 16 kHz spur is digital (fs/3, TIM17 16 kHz VCOS counter, or alias); test at 32 kHz. (9) hub brownout: unlikely. (10) VCOS register 0x92 (default 256) as an independent level meter, VCOSSTATE in 0xD0. (11) TRS contact / seating; wiggle test. Also: 0xD0 = 0 is normal outside capture; read during capture and expect RECSTATE = 2.
